About Me

As a medical student at Capital University (formerly Helwan University), I bring a unique, insider perspective to healthcare data. I combine my clinical understanding of the medical field with strong analytical skills to transform raw information into strategic assets.

My foundation in data analysis was rigorously developed through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), building upon my achievements as a graduate of the inaugural cohort of the Digital Egypt Cubs Initiative (DECI).

Proficient in Power BI, Microsoft Excel, and Power Query, I specialize in cleaning complex datasets and designing intuitive, interactive dashboards. My goal is to empower medical professionals and clinics to uncover hidden operational bottlenecks, track growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions that ultimately enhance both practice efficiency and patient outcomes.
